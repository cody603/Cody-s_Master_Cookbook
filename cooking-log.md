# The Cooking Log

**What we actually cooked, and when.** This is the file that answers *"what haven't we had in a while?"*

Add a row every time something gets cooked. Newest at the top. That's the whole job.

**Companions:** [Recipe Directory](recipe-directory.md) · [Difficulty Ladder](difficulty-ladder.md) · [Kitchen Companion](kitchen-companion.md)

---

## How to add to it

Just say it in chat — *"we did the pulled pork tonight"* — and it gets logged with today's date. Or add the row by hand.

**Columns:** the date it was cooked · the recipe, linked to the cookbook · who cooked it · how it went.

The **how it went** column is the valuable one over time. *"Too salty, cut the rub next time"* is worth more a year from
now than a star rating.

---

## Ask it anything

- *"What haven't we had in a while?"* — anything not cooked in 60+ days, family favourites first.
- *"What have we made this month?"*
- *"We're in a rut — what's a ❤️ we haven't touched since spring?"*
- *"What did we think of the ribs last time?"*
- *"Have I ever actually made this one?"*
- *"What have the kids cooked?"*

**How that gets answered:** compare this log against the [Recipe Directory](recipe-directory.md). A recipe with no row here
has never been logged — which is not the same as never cooked, just never written down. Say which it is rather than
guessing.
