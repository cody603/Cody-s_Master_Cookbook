# When this fires — and when it doesn't

**Fire it every time the conversation is about food cooked or eaten at home.** A missed trigger loses a revision or a plan; a spare one costs a small file fetch. When in doubt, fire.

**It fires on:**

- **The cookbook, named any way** — my cookbook, the family cookbook, read it, pull it, check it, add this to it, "in my cookbook I had some pho."
- **Planning meals** — "plan my week," "meal plan," "what should we have for dinner this week," "Monday we're having pulled pork," "I want street tacos Tuesday," "dinner's at seven," "let's do the brisket Saturday," "the fridge list," "the staple list," "the meal planning sheet," "what's for dinner" when we mean cooking.
- **Groceries** — grocery list, shopping list, what do we need, what to buy, push it to my reminders, send Vicky the list, a Hong Kong Market run, a Walmart order, "we're out of…"
- **Any house dish by name, or any recipe question** — pho, Darcy's steak, Geoff's pork belly, pulled pork, street tacos, brisket, Cody's chili, chili mac, Frito pie, jambalaya, gumbo, Crawfish Elegante, the smoked turkey, beer can chicken, sous vide chicken, Memphis Dust, Dalmatian rub, Captain Mike's, caulimash, Mamma's spinach, the Caesar — and anything else in the book. "What's up with Darcy's steak?" "How do we do the ribs?" "What's our easiest dinner?" "What's quick?" "What's keto?" "What needs the grill?"
- **Cooking right now** — "I'm making the chili," "walk me through the pho," "what temperature," "how long," "next step," "is it done," "what's the internal temp," "which rub."
- **Feedback on a meal we made — every time, without exception** — "too much salt in the pho," "that was too spicy," "the pork belly tasted boiled," "next time less garlic," "we loved it," "thumbs up," "that's a favorite," "the kids wouldn't eat it," "change the cookbook," "note that," "update the recipe." These get written down as proposals; never let one slide by.
- **Adding or changing a recipe** — "add this," a photo of a recipe or a card, "here's my mom's recipe," "write this up," "put this in the cookbook," "I'd rather sous vide that."
- **Leftovers and the freezer** — "we have leftover pork," "leftover meat," "frozen pulled pork," "do I need to thaw it," "I'll sous vide an extra chuck roast."
- **Timing and lead time** — "when do I need to start," "when should I put the brisket on," "dry brine tonight?", "how far ahead do I sous vide," "what time do I light the smoker," "put it on my calendar."
- **Shopping problems and substitutions** — "Walmart didn't have serrano peppers," "I used a jalapeño instead," "the almond flour chips were too expensive," "we couldn't find Thai basil."
- **Household groceries that aren't recipes** — "add Nutella and croissants," "we need more fruit," "sandwiches this week," "cottage cheese," "berries for the barbecue."
- **The rubs, sauces, and techniques** — "what goes on the ribs," "which sauce for the fish," "how do I make a roux," "what's the egg soak," "sous vide temp for chuck."

**It does not fire on:**

- **Eating out** — restaurants, "where should we eat," "what's good in Columbia, Missouri," reservations, takeout, delivery, DoorDash, menus.
- **Food that isn't ours to cook** — a restaurant dish we're not making, a grocery store's hours or location, kitchen-appliance shopping, general diet or medical nutrition advice with no dish behind it.
- **"What should we eat?" when the context is going out.** If it's unclear whether we mean cooking at home or going out, fire anyway and ask which — one question, then proceed.

# What to do when it fires

Before responding, fetch and follow the cookbook's chat instructions, live from GitHub:
