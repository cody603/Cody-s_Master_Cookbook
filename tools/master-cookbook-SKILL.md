# The trigger — if in doubt, fire

Fire this on **any implication that someone is going to cook, eat at home, shop for food, or plan meals.** A missed trigger loses a recipe change that has to be captured every time; a spare one costs one small fetch. When it could go either way, fire and ask one question.

**Fires on** the cookbook or the fridge list by any name · planning a day or a week ("Monday we're having pulled pork," "what should we have for dinner," "I want street tacos Tuesday," "dinner's at seven") · grocery lists, shopping lists, what to buy, a Walmart order, pushing a list to reminders · **any dish by name, even mangled by voice** — pho or *foe*, sous vide or *suvita*, caulimash or *calimash*, Tony Chachere's or *Tony Sattery's*, Frito pie or *freedo pie*, Prudhomme or *Purdom*, and every other recipe in the book · cooking something right now (how long, what temp, is it done, next step, which rub) · **feedback on a meal we made, every time without exception** ("too much salt in the pho," "that was too spicy," "we loved it," "thumbs up," "next time less garlic," "change the cookbook") · adding or changing a recipe, a photo of a recipe, "write this up" · leftovers, frozen meat, thawing, "I'll sous vide an extra chuck roast" · timing and lead time ("when do I start," "dry brine tonight?", "when do I light the smoker") · shopping problems and substitutions ("Walmart didn't have serrano," "I used a jalapeño") · rubs, sauces, techniques ("how do I make a roux," "what goes on the ribs") · household groceries that aren't recipes ("add Nutella and croissants," "we need fruit," "sandwiches this week").

**Does not fire on** restaurants, eating out, takeout, delivery, reservations, "what's good to eat in Columbia" — food that isn't ours to cook. **"What should we eat?" is ambiguous: fire, and ask once whether that means cooking at home or going out.**

# What to do when it fires

Before responding, fetch and follow the cookbook's chat manual, live from GitHub:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cody603/Cody-s_Master_Cookbook/main/CHAT.md

It is short. It tells you to fetch one small file first, the fridge sheet, and answer from that; how to match names by sound; how to speak in voice; and when the real work starts. Never rely on memory or an old copy, and never ask anyone to upload anything. The manual is kept current in the repo, so this skill never needs to change.
